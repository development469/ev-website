and the design philosophy

ev means home.

A father–daughter brand built on brass, craft, and the belief that objects should carry a human hand.

The beginning

Born of brass and design.

ev is a new brand in formation which respects authentic “Made in India” — history and craftsmanship, with attention to every small detail.

It is the brainchild of a father and a daughter. One a veteran in the brass industry, the other a luxury-marketing professional in the capital, New Delhi.

Jimmy (Ajay Gupta) is founder of Paragon Metal Industries — a manufacturer and exporter of brass handicrafts from India for over 30 years.

Adi (Aditi Gupta) started as a TV journalist, then a luxury marketer, and now ev — another dream to promote the artisans of India.

earth & vigor

The meaning behind ev

ev joins two words — earth (your “space”) and vigor (your “strength”, “energy” and “force”). Through its collection, ev intends to connect your earth with your vigor.

The literal meaning of ‘ev’ in Turkish is Home.

Why ev?

ev Vision

ev is for those who are not satisfied to have an ordinary product in their home, but search for authentic quality that also contains history, craftsmanship, and is designed with the individual in mind.

When you buy ev’s artisan objects, you are not just buying an item — you are buying a piece of the artist. You are a supporter, a fan, a collector of art.
